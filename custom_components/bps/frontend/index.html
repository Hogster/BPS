Receiver Calibration

❓ Compares the distances receivers measure between each other (each probe advertises an iBeacon) with their placed positions, and fits a per-receiver distance correction. Select the floor first, then sample while the house is quiet.
❓ Continuously samples in the background, re-solves every 15 minutes for every floor, and re-applies corrections whenever they change. Survives Home Assistant restarts.

Receiver placement

❓ Shows how precisely BPS can position someone across each floor with the receivers you have, and where one or two extra receivers would help most. Uses your floor plan, the receiver calibration above and the tracker zone history. Nothing is changed.

Where would one more receiver help most? Red areas are where BPS can't pin people down well; the stars are the best spots for a new receiver, ranked by how much they improve precision where people actually spend time.

How is this calculated?

Distance. A receiver's distance estimate is off by a roughly fixed fraction of the distance, so far-away receivers add little. How big that fraction is comes from your own receiver calibration: receivers measure each other at known distances, which captures walls, floors and mounting. A receiver whose measurements are consistently worse than the rest gets less weight.

Geometry. Being close to receivers is not enough; they need to surround you. Three receivers in a row on one side give a poor fix even when they are close, much like GPS satellites bunched in one part of the sky. The map combines distance, number of receivers in range and their spread around each point into one expected error.

Where people are. Each zone is weighted by how long trackers stayed there (recorder history of the tracker zone sensors), so a better fix in the kitchen counts more than in a storage room. Short visits that bounce straight back to the previous zone are listed as a reality check: they are what imprecision looks like in daily use.

Suggestions. A virtual receiver is tried at every metre inside the zones; the spot that lowers the time-weighted error most wins, then the best second spot given the first. Stars can land in a spot that's impractical (a balcony inside a zone, the middle of a room): use the nearest wall with power.

Reading the numbers. The metres come from a simplified model and are on the pessimistic side; the ranking and the relative improvement are what to act on. Re-run after moving or adding a receiver and recalibrating.

BPS is managed by an administrator

Floor plans, calibration and settings can only be changed by a Home Assistant administrator. An administrator can give everyone access under Settings → Access.

Follow me buttons for the dashboard

Each person gets sensor.bps_follow_<person>: available while music plays in the zone they are in and nobody else owns it, active while music follows them, otherwise off. When music starts near someone, BPS also asks in a notification. The card below shows Follow me only to the person it is for, and only while it is available, and Stop following while it is active. The buttons call bps.follow_me_start / bps.follow_me_stop, which act for whoever presses them. Persons need a Home Assistant user and at least one tracker (see Persons below); the YAML is built from your Persons settings, so save those first.

Notifications

                    ❓
                        Where the Home Assistant app opens when someone taps a BPS notification. Put a card for open questions on that page, so a question is never lost if the notification is dismissed.
                    
                

                
                
                
BPS asks its questions as notifications in the Home Assistant app. A dismissed or missed notification must not lose the question, so BPS also keeps the open question in sensor.bps_suggestion (state ask while a question waits, with title and text). Add the card below to the page above: it only shows while a question is open, and the buttons answer as the person who presses them.

                

                    Dashboard card (YAML)
                    
Dashboard → Edit → Add card → Manual, paste, save.

                    

                        
                        
type: conditional
conditions:
  - condition: state
    entity: sensor.bps_suggestion
    state: ask
card:
  type: vertical-stack
  cards:
    - type: markdown
      content: |-
        ### {{ state_attr('sensor.bps_suggestion', 'title') }}
        {{ state_attr('sensor.bps_suggestion', 'text') }}
    - type: horizontal-stack
      cards:
        - type: button
          name: "Yes"
          icon: mdi:check
          tap_action:
            action: perform-action
            perform_action: bps.suggestion_answer
            data:
              answer: "yes"
        - type: button
          name: "No"
          icon: mdi:close
          tap_action:
            action: perform-action
            perform_action: bps.suggestion_answer
            data:
              answer: "no"
        - type: button
          name: Don't ask again
          icon: mdi:cancel
          tap_action:
            action: perform-action
            perform_action: bps.suggestion_answer
            data:
              answer: never
